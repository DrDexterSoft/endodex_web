Cancellations and refunds

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Software provider: Victor José Oviedo Caldarera, operating in Paraguay under the EndoDEX brand. Contact: endodex@dr-dex.net.

1. Scope

Canceling a renewal and requesting a refund are separate actions. Registration and the initial 7-day trial require no card and trigger no automatic charge when the trial ends. Paddle purchases are processed by Paddle as reseller and merchant of record. Apple, Google and other store purchases must also follow the relevant store’s procedures.

2. How to cancel

Sign in to EndoDEX, open subscriptions and select Manage subscription and invoices. Confirm cancellation in the Paddle portal, use the management link in your receipt, or contact Paddle purchase support. Normal cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period and prevents later renewals. Check the effective date and keep the confirmation. Uninstalling the app, stopping use or deleting an account is not cancellation.

3. Changes between monthly and annual

Changes that the app allows you to schedule start after the paid cycle ends. The confirmation shows the new amount and effective date; scheduling does not collect a payment. Where a payment method requires fresh authorization, it must be completed before the change can be scheduled.

4. Commercial refund policy

EndoDEX offers a full refund of the first subscription payment if you request it within 14 calendar days of that payment. This voluntary guarantee applies to the initial purchase of either the monthly or annual plan, including if you have used the paid features. The period starts with the first payment, not account registration or the free trial. Renewal payments and requests outside this period are reviewed individually, with no additional automatic refund guarantee, without limiting any mandatory rights. Use the contact methods below and identify the transaction.

5. Buyer rights and requests

Mandatory withdrawal, refund, warranty and other applicable rights remain unaffected, including rights concerning defective or misdescribed products. A free trial does not remove them. Paddle transactions also follow its refund policy and Buyer Terms. Contact endodex@dr-dex.net, the support link in your receipt or Paddle with your account email, transaction date/ID and a brief explanation. Do not include full payment credentials or identifiable patient data.

6. Processing

Approved refunds are processed through the payment provider or store. Methods and timing depend on that channel and applicable obligations. A refund may affect or end access linked to the purchase; its effect will be explained when the request is handled. A refund inquiry does not replace cancellation of future renewals. If you do not want to continue, also cancel and verify the subscription status.

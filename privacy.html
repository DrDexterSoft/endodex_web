Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Software provider: Victor José Oviedo Caldarera, operating in Paraguay under the EndoDEX brand. Contact: endodex@dr-dex.net.

1. Scope

This notice covers the account, application, support and billing. You or your organization must determine the purpose and appropriate authorization or legal basis for clinical information entered in professional practice. Local storage remains on your device or browser; using cloud storage transmits case data to the service. Creating an account also involves personal data, even during a free trial.

2. Information processed

Account information includes user ID, email, name when available, sign-in provider and account/access dates. Case information includes case codes and the clinical history, findings, classifications, results and pain maps you enter. Billing records include customer, transaction and subscription IDs, plan, status, dates and payment confirmations. Support messages and technical records may be processed to operate and protect the service and resolve issues. Never send full card numbers, security codes or passwords to support.

3. Purposes

Information is used to authenticate users, provide requested features, store and retrieve cases you choose to save, verify payments, provide support, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. Depending on the context, processing relates to providing the requested service, legal obligations, service protection or the authorization required for the data concerned.

4. Local and cloud storage

The 7-day trial includes local case storage, not cloud storage. A paid subscription enables cloud features; cases saved there are linked to your account. Local files and exports can remain on your device after sign-out. Clearing browser data, reinstalling or changing devices may affect local records. Protect your devices and manage your copies and exports.

5. Providers and international processing

EndoDEX uses Google Firebase for authentication, database and infrastructure. Google or Apple participates in authentication if you choose that sign-in method. Paddle processes billing data for Paddle purchases under its privacy policy. Other stores or payment providers apply their own terms. These services may process information in countries other than yours; storage exclusively in Paraguay or on your device is not guaranteed.

6. Browser storage, retention and deletion

Local storage and session mechanisms support the app, saved cases and sign-in; authentication and payment components may use their own necessary mechanisms. Blocking them may prevent access or payment. Account and cloud case data are retained to provide requested services, subject to deletion requests and applicable obligations. Available controls allow case and account deletion requests. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a subscription or erase payment-provider records. Transaction, dispute and legally required records may be retained as necessary. Instant removal of every technical copy is not promised. Delete local files and exports on the devices where you stored them, and cancel renewal before deleting your account.

7. Your requests and patient information

Contact endodex@dr-dex.net to request information, access, correction or deletion and exercise any other applicable rights. Identity verification may be needed; do not include identifiable clinical information or passwords. If a professional or institution entered your patient data, contact them first to help identify the records and address your request; this does not limit your applicable rights.

8. Security and updates

Use trusted devices and protect your account. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Report incidents without attaching unnecessary clinical information. The date on this page identifies the latest version; material changes will be communicated as appropriate where required.
